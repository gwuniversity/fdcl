His research brings differential geometry and applied mathematics to control, estimation, optimization, and artificial intelligence for robotics and aerospace systems. His work spans mathematical theory, computational methods, and experimental validation through UAV flight experiments, with interests including geometric control, uncertainty propagation on manifolds, and geometry-informed learning.

He is the lead principal investigator of an AFOSR Multidisciplinary University Research Initiative (MURI) developing theoretical and computational foundations for open hybrid dynamical systems (2023–2028).
