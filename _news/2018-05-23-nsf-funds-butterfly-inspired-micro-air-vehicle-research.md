The National Science Foundation awarded FDCL funding for **“Dynamics and Control of Long Range Micro Air Vehicles Inspired by Monarch Butterflies.”**

The project studies monarch butterfly biomechanics to inform the design and control of flapping-wing unmanned aerial vehicles, connecting biological flight with engineering models and control methods.
