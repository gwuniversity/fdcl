FDCL has been awarded funding through the Office of Naval Research’s Science of AI program for **CHASE: Cultivating Human-AI Synergy via Decentralized Elicitation and Learning**.

The project brings together Taeyoung Lee and Tian Lan at The George Washington University and Mahdi Imani at Northeastern University to investigate learning and collaboration between people and AI systems.
