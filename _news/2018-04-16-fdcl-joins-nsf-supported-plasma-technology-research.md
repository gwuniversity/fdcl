FDCL is participating in the NSF Industry–University Cooperative Research Centers program through GW’s Center for High Pressure Plasma Energy, Agriculture, and Biomedical Technologies.

The lab’s contribution focuses on modeling and control of plasma technologies, including research on efficacy and safety in biological processes and adaptive approaches to plasma-based cancer treatment.
