Tejaswi K. C. presented his research on imitation learning at IROS 2022 in Kyoto, Japan.

The work, **“Constrained imitation learning for a flapping wing unmanned aerial vehicle,”** studies learning-based control for flapping-wing flight while accounting for constraints. The paper appeared in *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2022.3194682).
