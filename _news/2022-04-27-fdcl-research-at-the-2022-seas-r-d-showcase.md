FDCL presented six projects at the 2022 SEAS Research and Development Showcase, spanning learning, perception, estimation, and autonomous flight.

- **Zichao Hou:** Reinforcement learning for adaptive plasma cancer treatment with electrochemical impedance spectroscopy.
- **Maneesha Wickramasuriya:** Deep image segmentation and object tracking for known 3D objects.
- **Beomyeol Yu:** Equivariant reinforcement learning for quadrotor UAVs.
- **Kanishke Gamagedara:** Autonomous shipboard landing of a UAV in a GPS-denied environment.
- **Weixin Wang:** Bayesian point-cloud registration with the matrix Fisher–Gaussian distribution.
- **Tejaswi K. C.:** Constrained imitation learning for a flapping-wing UAV.

The lab shared photographs of the showcase on April 26 and the research posters on April 27.
