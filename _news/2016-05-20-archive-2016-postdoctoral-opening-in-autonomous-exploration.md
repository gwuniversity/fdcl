In May 2016, FDCL advertised a postdoctoral position in autonomous exploration. The project focused on motion planning and control for multiple aerial vehicles cooperatively exploring an unknown area, drawing on estimation, mapping, SLAM, and flight experiments.

**This is an archived recruitment announcement.** For current opportunities, visit [Join the Lab]({{ "/open_position/" | relative_url }}).
