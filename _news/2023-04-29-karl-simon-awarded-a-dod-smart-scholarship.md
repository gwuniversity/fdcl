Congratulations to Karl Simon on receiving a Department of Defense SMART Scholarship. The Scholarship-for-Service program supports the development of the civilian science and engineering workforce at DoD facilities.

As described in the lab’s announcement, the award provides tuition support, a stipend, summer internship experience, and a pathway to employment at a DoD facility.
