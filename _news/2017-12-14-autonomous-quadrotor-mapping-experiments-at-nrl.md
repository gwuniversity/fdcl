Evan Kaufman and Kuya Takami conducted flight experiments at the Naval Research Laboratory on autonomous quadrotor exploration and mapping. FDCL shared their video, **“Autonomous Quadrotor 3D Mapping and Exploration Using Exact Occupancy Probabilities.”**

The demonstration connects probabilistic mapping with aerial exploration. The original announcement provides the video.
