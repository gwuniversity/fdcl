FDCL received a grant through the National Science Foundation’s Cyber-Physical Systems program for **“Edge-Cloud Support for Predictable, Global Situational-Awareness for Autonomous Vehicles.”**

The project connects autonomous-vehicle research with computing support for situational awareness.

[NSF award 1837382](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=1837382).
