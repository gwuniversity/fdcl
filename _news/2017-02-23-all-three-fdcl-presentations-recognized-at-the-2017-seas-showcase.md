Congratulations to Mahdis Bisheban, Evan Kaufman, and Shankar Kulumani on receiving awards at the 2017 GW SEAS Research and Development Showcase. All three presentations from FDCL were recognized.

The lab also looked back at Shankar and Chris’s first-place award at the 2016 showcase, celebrating a second year of student research recognition.
