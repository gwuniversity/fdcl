FDCL highlighted Tejaswi K. C.’s work on **“Dynamics and Control of a Flapping Wing UAV with Abdomen Undulation Inspired by Monarch Butterfly.”**

The demonstration explores how abdomen motion can be incorporated into the dynamics and control of a flapping-wing vehicle. The original announcement provides the associated demonstration.
