Congratulations to FDCL alumna Mahdis Bisheban on beginning a tenure-track Assistant Professor position in the Department of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering at the University of Calgary.

The lab also shared a National Research Council Canada Women in Engineering feature about Dr. Bisheban. We celebrate both her professional recognition and this new faculty appointment.
