Evan Kaufman, Mahdis Bisheban, and Kanishke Gamagedara conducted flight experiments at GW’s Science and Engineering Hall. FDCL shared the results in a video titled **“Autonomous Aerial 3D Exploration and Mapping.”**

The demonstration links aerial robotics, mapping, and exploration in a physical indoor environment. The video is available through the original announcement.
