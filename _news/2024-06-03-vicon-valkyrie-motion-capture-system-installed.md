FDCL’s new Vicon Valkyrie motion capture system is operational. The installation expands the lab’s experimental infrastructure for tracking motion during robotics and flight experiments.

Special thanks to Beomyeol (Ben) Yu and Maneesha (Maneesh) Wickramasuriya for their work on the system’s design, acquisition, and installation.
